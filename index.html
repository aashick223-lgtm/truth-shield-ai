🔐 Golden Safety Rules

  • Never share OTP, PIN, or passwords.
  • Never install apps at a stranger's request.
  • Do not transfer money because of threats.
  • Verify news with multiple reliable sources.
  • Ask a trusted person if you are unsure.

🚨 Report Cyber Fraud (India)

If money has been lost to cyber fraud, contact the official helpline immediately.

Cyber Fraud Helpline: 1930

Official website: cybercrime.gov.in

Use official channels. Never share your OTP with anyone claiming to help.
