Scam Call Safety

Stay calm. Protect your details.

🔐
Never share OTP or PIN
Banks will not ask you to reveal your secret PIN or OTP over a call.
⛔
Don't rush into action
Scammers may threaten you or promise prizes to pressure you.
☎️
Verify independently
Hang up and contact the organisation using its official number.
🛡️
Act quickly if money is lost
Contact your bank and India's cybercrime helpline at 1930.
💡 Tip: Never install screen-sharing apps or reveal passwords at the request of an unknown caller.
🆘

Cyber Report & Help

If you suspect cybercrime, use official channels to report it and seek help.

🚨 Report Cybercrime 📞 Call Helpline 1930

🇮🇳 For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 as soon as possible and report through the official portal. Never share OTPs or banking PINs.
